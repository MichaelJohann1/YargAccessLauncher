# Changelog

11/27/2026 (0.1.4):

- The Documents tab has the YargAccess 0.1.3 changelog: calibrator fixes for controllers and Back, and the new Setting your timing page in Getting Started.

11/27/2026 (0.1.3):

- The song preview's Listen and Repeat matches YargAccess 0.1.2 at time signature changes: each listen bar is as long as the bar you answer.
- The song preview follows the Mono Sound setting.
- Fixed: after you closed a document on the Documents tab, Tab moved into an empty page (about:blank) that you could not leave.

11/20/2026 (0.1.2):

- The song preview's Listen and Repeat now matches YargAccess 0.1.1: patterns are whole bars that start on a bar line, and Call Length is 1, 2 or 4 bars, 2 by default.

11/18/2026 (0.1.1):

- The Download songs through Chorus tab lists the newest charts when it first opens, so you can browse without searching. Browse all charts clears the search boxes and lists every chart in the chosen order, and Go to page jumps to a page number.
- Search and browse results no longer list the same chart twice. Chorus Encore sends a chart once for every pack it is in.

11/17/2026 (0.1.0):

- Initial test release of the YARG Access launcher.
