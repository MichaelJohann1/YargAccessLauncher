# Changelog

11/27/2026 (0.1.4):

- Tutorial practice and Practice The Buttons list the computer keyboard as a device. When more than one device could be played on, such as the keyboard and a controller, they ask which one to use instead of picking the controller. The device you pressed last is first in the list.
- The read me and the Getting Started tutorial say how the device is chosen.

11/27/2026 (0.1.3):

- The calibrator works with a controller or guitar that has no profile. Every button counts as a tap except B, Red and View or Select, and the prompt now says which button to avoid.
- Back while the calibrator waits for your first press no longer starts the clicks for a moment, and stopping the clicks early turns YARG's Whammy FX setting back on.
- Strum, the d-pad and the arrow keys no longer read the help list while the clicks play. The calibration result is spoken in your language.
- The Getting Started tutorial has a new page, Setting your timing, before the song library, that walks you through the calibrator. The Settings tutorial now points to it.

11/27/2026 (0.1.2):

- Listen and Repeat handles time signature changes. Each listen bar is now as long as the bar you answer, so the calls stay on the beat in songs with time signature changes. Where the bar length changes, a pattern gets shorter or starts a bar later.
- New on the YargAccess tab: Mono Sound plays every cue from the centre, for players who hear with one ear or use one speaker. Pitch still tells the lanes apart. The Settings tutorial has a page on it.

11/20/2026 (0.1.1):

- Listen and Repeat patterns are now whole bars of music and always start on a bar line, instead of a number of notes. Call Length is 1, 2 or 4 bars. 2 bars is the new default and recommended, and a length you saved before this update goes back to 2 once.
- New on the YargAccess tab: Duck Music During Listen makes the song quieter while a Listen and Repeat pattern plays, and Ducking Amount sets how much quieter. It is off at first.
- The Getting Started tutorial was rewritten as one walk-through in a clearer order: the basics, getting songs, profiles and game modes, how a song works, the play styles, the song library, and playing with others or with bots.
- Drums: if YARG gives no lane positions, the pads now play from fixed places, left to right, instead of all from the centre.
- Replays and Listen and Repeat best scores from before this update keep their old note groups. History reads them as notes and new ones as bars.

11/17/2026 (0.1.0):

- Initial test release of YargAccess.
